About MedEvidence Clinical AI

Built to make clinical research, medication safety, and prescription understanding fast, transparent, and accessible.

What Our Website Does

MedEvidence bridges the gap between complex medical literature and fast everyday clinical lookup. Unlike generic chatbots that rely solely on static training data, our LangGraph agent actively queries live biomedical registries to verify symptoms, FDA drug warnings, and clinical trials.

  • Instant 1-Word Disease Lookup: Type a single condition name when short on time to get a structured clinical summary.
  • Prescription Image & PDF Vision: Upload a prescription photo or PDF to detect the underlying condition and extract every tablet name, dosage, and schedule.
  • Natural Conversational Flow: Responds warmly to greetings and follow-up questions with context-aware clinical prompts.
